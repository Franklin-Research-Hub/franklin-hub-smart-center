# The Living Canvas™ and The STEM Studio (future build-out app)

Built and designed by Victoria Love Franklin, Founder.

This app is kept in the repository for a future release. It is not linked from the website, is excluded from the sitemap, and tells search engines not to index it (robots.txt also disallows `/future-apps/`).

- `index.html`: The Living Canvas™, 19 diverse 3D portraits (Different Faces. Different Perspectives. One Interconnected World.), customize panel, guided tour, Export PNG, and The Canvas Collection.
- `stem-studio.html`: The STEM Studio, with 9 tools in the Customize Your Canvas panel and 9 challenges.
- Shared files it needs stay in the main site folders: `assets/js/living-canvas.js`, `assets/js/three.module.min.js`, `assets/js/OrbitControls.js`, and `assets/canvas/`.

Preview after publishing: `https://franklinhubsmartcenter.com/future-apps/living-canvas/`

To launch it later, link to this folder from the Arts & Design page, or move the two pages back to the site root.
