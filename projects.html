Wastewater surveillanceGenomic surveillanceGeospatial analyticsMachine learningRespiratory virus forecastingPublic-health equity
STATUS5 papers acceptedICEPTP 2027 · London, UKApril 8–10, 2027
The Helix in action

Four of the five Knowledge Helix™ pathways light up in this project. Each one is backed by the published research.

DT ↔ INData ↔ InsightRS ↔ DSResearch ↔ DiscoveryAI ↔ IVAI ↔ InnovationCR ↔ SLCreativity ↔ SolutionsED ↔ IMEducation ↔ Impact
  • DT ↔ INWastewater, genomic, geospatial, and clinical data are brought together to reveal infectious-disease signals, surveillance coverage, and public-health patterns. Papers: #117, #134
  • RS ↔ DSResearch questions become evidence: five papers accepted to the 12th International Conference on Environmental Pollution, Treatment and Protection (ICEPTP 2027). Papers: #117, #133, #134, #135, #136
  • AI ↔ IVMachine-learning models and probabilistic forecasts test how well wastewater signals anticipate respiratory-virus hospitalizations. Papers: #133, #135
  • ED ↔ IMFindings on public-health equity and on the limits of wastewater surveillance, such as observable population and lead time, inform communities and decision-makers. Papers: #117, #136
